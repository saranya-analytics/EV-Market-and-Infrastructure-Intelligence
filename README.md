Electric Vehicle Sales & Infrastructure Analytics (Power BI)

Project Overview:
An interactive 3-page Power BI dashboard analyzing India's EV market growth (4M+ registrations) alongside charging infrastructure density (12K+ PCS) across 34 states.

Key Dashboard Pages
Overview: Executive summary featuring KPI metrics, geographic sales distribution, and top manufacturers.
Sales Trends: Category market share breakdown (2W, 3W, LMV, MMV) and YoY growth trends.
Infrastructure: State-level operational charging station density and demand alignment.

Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Data Modeling (Star Schema)
- DAX Measures
- Custom Navigation & Dynamic Slicers
